## Recommendations

- **Improve campaign targeting:** Use customer characteristics, income segments, and purchasing behavior to identify higher-potential customer groups.

- **Explore digital conversion opportunities:** Investigate the difference in web purchasing behavior between responders and non-responders, and test personalized offers or targeted campaigns to improve conversion.

- **Prioritize high-value customers:** Develop retention and loyalty strategies for customers identified among the highest spenders.

- **Optimize channel strategy:** Maintain the Store channel as a core sales channel given its highest purchase volume, while exploring opportunities to increase Web and Catalog purchases.

- **Personalize customer experiences:** Tailor offers and messaging based on customer characteristics and historical purchasing behavior, and evaluate their effectiveness through campaign performance metrics.
